Research Overview

Current Work

With Prof. Elena I. Bunina at Bar-Ilan University, I am classifying the automorphisms of twisted Chevalley groups of several types (\(^2A_\ell\), \(^2D_\ell\), \(^2E_6\), \(^3D_4\)) over local and commutative rings — extending automorphism theory beyond the untwisted case.

Past Projects

My doctoral work at IIT Bombay, with Prof. Shripad M. Garge, studied the structure of twisted Chevalley groups over commutative rings: their normal subgroups, the normalizer of the group inside its automorphism group, and triangular/unitriangular factorizations.
