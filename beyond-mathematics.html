Some Useful Links

▸News & Conference Updates

Group Theory News

A running calendar of group theory conferences and workshops around the world, kept by the journal Advances in Group Theory and Applications.

RepresentationTheory.ca

Upcoming conferences and meetings in representation theory, with speaker lists.

The Geometric Group Theory Page

Jon McCammond’s long-running directory for geometric group theory and low-dimensional topology — people, organizations, conferences, journals and problem lists.

▸Blogs & Expository Writing

Terence Tao’s blog, What’s new

Expository notes, lecture material and research updates across a wide sweep of mathematics.

▸Tools & Interactive Demos

Simple Lie Algebra Roots & Weights

An interactive demo: give it a type \(X_N\) and a highest weight and it returns the Cartan matrix, the Dynkin diagram, and the weights of the corresponding irreducible module — with a plot in ranks up to three.

Matrix calculator

Determinants, inverses, ranks, eigenvalues and row reduction, worked step by step in the browser — handy for checking a computation by hand.
