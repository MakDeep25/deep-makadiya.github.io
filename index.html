About Me

I am a Post-doctoral Fellow at Bar-Ilan University, working with Prof. Elena I. Bunina on the automorphisms of twisted Chevalley groups over local and commutative rings. My doctoral work at IIT Bombay, under Prof. Shripad M. Garge, focused on the structure of twisted Chevalley groups — their normal subgroups, normalizers, and factorizations.
